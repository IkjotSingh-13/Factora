03

How to read a result

  1. Stance direction — do the verified quotes point toward or away from the claim. −1 all against, +1 all for.
  2. Evidence strength — how good that evidence is: credibility, relevance, agreement across independent domains, freshness, context. Not a probability that the claim is true.
  3. Verdict — both combined, with an explicit INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE below two independent sources.
  4. Audit trail — every counter, every stage’s milliseconds, every failed source.
