Prepared inputs each exercise one outcome: a confirmed figure, a contradicted health claim, a right fact with the wrong scope, and evidence too thin to judge. Nothing here returns a verdict without cited, quote-verified sources.

How to read a result

Stance direction — do the verified quotes point toward or away from the claim. Ranges −1 (all evidence against) to +1 (all for).

Evidence strength — how good that evidence is: credibility, relevance, independence, freshness, context. It is not a probability the claim is true, and a claim can have strong evidence against it.

Verdict — the two combined, with an explicit INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE when fewer than two independent sources carry a stance.
